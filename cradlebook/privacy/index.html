Privacy Policy

Baby log for feeds, diapers and sleep

Provider: Albert John Alcantara, publishing as CNTRL Design (“we”, “us”)
Last updated: 23 October 2026
Contact: support@cntrldesign.com

Cradlebook is a log you keep about your baby: feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping, the milk stash, growth and medications. This policy explains where that log is kept. We built Cradlebook so that we never see it.

The short version: Your log lives on your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We have no server, no accounts and no analytics. We cannot see, read or recover your log.
01

What Cradlebook stores

Everything you enter: your baby’s name and birth date, feeds, diapers (with any photo or note you add), sleep, pumping sessions, the milk stash, growth measurements, medications and the doses you log. It is saved on your iPhone. If you delete an entry, a baby, or the app, it is erased from your iPhone.

02

Information that leaves your iPhone

Your iCloud (Apple)

If you are signed in to iCloud, Cradlebook keeps a copy of your log in your private iCloud database so it is backed up and stays in step across your devices. This uses Apple’s iCloud under your Apple Account. We cannot access your iCloud data. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the log stays only on your iPhone.

Sharing with a partner

If you choose Share with your partner, the people you invite can see, add, change and delete entries in the shared family log. The log stays in your iCloud; Apple delivers it to the people you invite. You can see who has access and stop sharing at any time in Cradlebook’s Settings.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Cradlebook checks your purchase on your iPhone. We never see your payment details, and Apple’s sales reports to us contain no personal information.

Reminders

Medication and milk reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server of ours.

03

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, health records or advertising identifiers. Cradlebook contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data. If you allow it in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us to help fix bugs.

04

Third-party services

Only Apple: iCloud (backup, sync and sharing, under your Apple Account) and the App Store (purchases). Each is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

05

Data retention

  • On your iPhone and in your iCloud: kept until you delete it. Deleting an entry or a baby in Cradlebook also removes it from your iCloud.
  • With us: nothing, because we never receive your log.
06

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete entries or a baby: swipe an entry to delete it; remove a baby from the baby switcher.
  • Delete everything in iCloud: iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Cradlebook → Delete Data. Then delete the app to remove the copy on your iPhone.
  • Stop sharing: Cradlebook → Settings → Manage sharing.
  • For any privacy question or request, email us at the address above.
07

Children

Cradlebook is for parents and caregivers, not for children. It stores information about your child that you choose to enter, and that information stays on your devices and in your iCloud as described above.

08

Health information

Cradlebook shows typical ranges from cited public sources as general information. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or check doses or interactions. If you are worried about your baby, contact your doctor, pharmacist or a nurse line.

09

Security

Your iPhone’s passcode and Apple’s iCloud encryption protect your log. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we keep nothing on our side to be breached.

10

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and note significant changes in the app’s release notes.
