Cradlebook Support

Baby log for feeds, diapers and sleep

Questions, a problem, or an idea? Email support@cntrldesign.com. A real person reads every message.

01

Sharing with your partner

Both of you need Cradlebook and an iPhone signed in to iCloud. On the iPhone that has the log, open Settings in Cradlebook (tap the baby’s picture) and choose Share with your partner. Send the invite in Messages. Your partner opens the link on their iPhone before adding a baby, and the shared log appears in a moment.

02

Free trial and unlock

Cradlebook is free to try for 7 days with everything unlocked. After that your history stays readable, and a one-time purchase unlocks logging again for good. There is no subscription. Bought it already? Settings → Restore purchases. Family Sharing is supported.

03

Backup and a new iPhone

When you are signed in to iCloud, your log is kept in your private iCloud and comes back when you install Cradlebook on a new iPhone with the same Apple Account.

04

Typical ranges

Typical ranges come from cited public health sources and are general information, not medical advice. If you are worried about your baby, contact your doctor or a nurse line.

05

Privacy

Your log stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud; we never see it. Read the privacy policy.
